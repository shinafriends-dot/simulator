ACT 01 — THE PROBLEM

Scotland generates more wind than the cable can carry.
The Smart Buffer absorbs the surplus.

When offshore wind generation exceeds export capacity, curtailment occurs — clean energy goes unused and constraint payments are recovered from consumer bills. The Smart Buffer Array is a controllable demand asset designed to absorb that surplus and convert it into productive output.

ACT 03 — THE SOLUTION

One container. One signal. The waste stops.

The Smart Buffer Array sits adjacent to Tealing Substation. When curtailment is detected it instantly absorbs surplus energy and converts it into productive compute output.

LIVE ENERGY FLOW — TEALING SUBSTATION
ACT 04 — THE 200ms RESPONSE

Faster than a protection relay.
Zero voltage shock to the network.

The Siemens S7-1200 PLC receives the curtailment signal and transfers the full 1MW load to the DC intermediate buffer layer — allowing a controlled, zero-impact soft ramp-down of the AC grid connection and preventing transient voltage spikes.
